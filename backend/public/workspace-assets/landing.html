About the concept

About the concept

What is Sume?

Sume connects creator campaign invitations, agreed terms and PayPal payment in one proposed workflow.

What do the agents handle?

They use supplied offers, availability and private limits to clarify campaign terms. Both people approve the agreement; the buyer separately authorizes payment.

What does this example show?

Fictional ChatEDU invites creators. Maya agrees to one Reel with 30-day usage for $700, receives a simulated PayPal payment and asks Sume to find her next business partners.

Where does PayPal fit?

The concept brings businesses and creators onto PayPal, then uses PayPal for approved campaign payments. Creator-led discovery could bring new counterparties and repeat payments into the same network.

Why could the network grow?

A business invites creators. Those creators return to find other businesses, which can invite more creators. More useful counterparties could encourage participation and repeat commerce. This is a hypothesis, not measured growth.

Is this live?

The film is simulated. In the interactive campaign, creator activity is simulated, agent recommendations run live, and payout uses PayPal Sandbox.
